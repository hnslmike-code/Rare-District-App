## PROMPT START

### 1. Bottom Navigation Bar (mobile)
A fixed bottom nav bar, visible on mobile widths, with 5 items:
- **Shop** — home/house icon, links to the homepage
- **Wardrobe** — Wardrobe icon, links to the user's saved/wardrobe items
- **Showroom** — door icon, opens the editorial/ journal
- **Vendor Hub** — tag icon, links to the vendor dashboard (if the logged-in user is a vendor) or to "Become a Vendor" signup (if they're a shopper)
- **Me** — person icon, links to account/profile

Style: glass panel (frosted, semi-transparent, subtle border), fixed to the bottom of the viewport, active item highlighted with a gold glow/underline, icons in chrome/off-white with the active icon in gold. Smooth transition when switching active state.
